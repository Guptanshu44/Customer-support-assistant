POLICY: Refund Policy
- Refunds are only applicable within 30 days of purchase.
- Refund processing takes 5-7 business days.
- Do NOT promise refunds outside the 30-day window without supervisor approval.
- Cash on Delivery orders are refunded via bank transfer only.
- Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable.

POLICY: Delivery Policy
- Standard delivery: 3-7 business days.
- Express delivery: 1-2 business days (available in select cities).
- Do NOT guarantee same-day delivery unless explicitly confirmed in the system.
- Do NOT promise delivery on Sundays or public holidays.
- International delivery: 10-15 business days.

POLICY: Return Policy
- Returns accepted within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging.
- Perishables, intimate wear, and customized products are non-returnable.
- Damaged items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.

POLICY: Escalation Policy
- Escalate to a senior agent if:
  - Customer has contacted support more than 3 times for the same issue.
  - Customer requests to speak to a manager.
  - Issue involves legal complaints or regulatory concerns.
  - Customer is threatening to go to consumer court.
- Do NOT promise escalation resolution timelines without supervisor confirmation.

POLICY: Discount and Coupon Policy
- Agents cannot create or issue ad-hoc discount codes.
- Only pre-approved coupons from the marketing team can be applied.
- Do NOT promise discounts to appease angry customers without supervisor approval.
- One coupon per order — multiple coupons cannot be stacked.

POLICY: Data and Privacy Policy
- Do NOT share customer personal data (address, phone, email) in the chat.
- Verify customer identity before accessing account details.
- Do NOT access accounts without customer's explicit permission.

POLICY: Communication Guidelines
- Always greet the customer by name if available.
- Use empathetic language — avoid defensive or dismissive tone.
- Do NOT use jargon or technical terms without explanation.
- Always confirm resolution before closing the chat.
- Do NOT make promises that are outside company policy.
